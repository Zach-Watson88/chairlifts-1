Chairlift is the ski and snowboard app for college riders in the Northeast who get out ten or more days a season. See what's open at your mountain, track every run, and get coached by AI from a short phone video of yourself.

The AI Riding Coach

A coach in your pocket after every run

  1. Film 10–30 seconds of yourself riding.A friend's phone from the side of the trail is all it takes.
  2. Upload the clip.The AI looks at your stance, balance, edging and turn shape frame by frame.
  3. Get 2–3 fixes, ranked.Each one comes with a drill and a short cue to think about on your next run.

Built for weekend riders

Chairlift is for college students and young skiers and snowboarders who ride mid-size mountains like Holiday Valley, Seven Springs and Killington on weekends, want to keep improving, and can't spend $100 on a lesson every time they go out.
