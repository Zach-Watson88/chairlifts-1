About Chairlift

One app for your whole day on the mountain

Chairlift is a mobile web app for skiers and snowboarders. Pick the mountain you're at to see which runs and lifts are open, when the lifts and lodges open and close, and today's snow and weather conditions. Track your speed and runs as you ride and count your days on snow across the season. The AI Riding Coach turns a short phone video of your run into a technique breakdown with drills for your next run, and you can rate and review mountains on a five-star scale.
